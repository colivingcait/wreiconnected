## Where do women find a real estate mentor?

Most of the useful mentoring our hosts talk about started at a women's real estate investing meetup. WREI Connected exists so that room is in more cities.

## What does a good mentor do?

A good mentor answers a specific deal question, introduces you when it helps, and does not ask you to recruit anyone. They still invest.

## What should you walk away from?

Walk away from guaranteed returns, pressure to wire money, and anyone who will not let you ask about the downside. Bring the story to a [WREI Connected meetup](/events) and let the room react.
