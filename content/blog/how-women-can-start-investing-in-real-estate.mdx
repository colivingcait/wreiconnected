## How does a beginner actually start?

Pick one strategy you can explain in a sentence, such as house hacking or a small rental. Then learn what a payment looks like in your city before you fall in love with a house.

## What should the first 90 days include?

A WREI Connected meetup, one conversation with a lender, and a written budget. Women real estate investors who skip the room usually learn the same lessons later, alone.

## Where do I meet people who have done it?

Find a [women's real estate investing meetup](/find) or read [how to find a mentor](/blog/finding-a-real-estate-mentor-as-a-woman).
