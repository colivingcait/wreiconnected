## What is a real estate investing group for women?

It is a room where women real estate investors compare notes in person. WREI Connected chapters are that room, run locally and connected nationally.

## How is WREI Connected different from a Facebook group?

A Facebook group can introduce you to the conversation. A WREI Connected meetup is where you meet the women doing deals in your city. The Quarterly Summit is the national online summit for women real estate investors.

Open [Find a Meetup](/find) or the [events calendar](/events). If your city is not listed, [start a chapter](/partner).
